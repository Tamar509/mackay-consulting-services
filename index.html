Our approach

One engagement, not three

Reform programmes are usually split three ways: a lawyer drafts the instrument, a technology team builds a system that only loosely reflects it, and an evaluator arrives at the end to measure something nobody designed for. The seams between those three workstreams are where implementation quietly fails.

MCS is deliberately structured to hold all three. We can draft the legal instrument, design the system that gives effect to it, and produce the evidence that it worked — under one workplan, one methodology and one line of accountability.
