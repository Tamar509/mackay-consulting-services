Our people

A small firm, built around three practitioners.

Mackay Consulting Services was incorporated in Sierra Leone in July 2026. Its directors have spent their careers in legislative drafting, public sector digitalisation and inclusive development finance, and they lead the work themselves rather than delegating it downwards.

A note for procurement teams: the experience set out below was accrued by each director personally, before and independently of the formation of the firm. It is presented as key personnel experience, and should be read as such. Where corporate experience is required separately, please contact us and we will say plainly where MCS stands.

Beyond the directors

Associates and consortium working

For larger assignments the directors draw on a panel of associate consultants — drafters, statisticians, sector specialists and enumerator teams — assembled for the requirements of the specific engagement. We are also open to consortium arrangements with international firms that need a Freetown-based partner with genuine drafting, systems and evaluation capability rather than a nominal local presence.
